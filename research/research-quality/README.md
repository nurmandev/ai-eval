## Scope boundary

This is research design and documentation only. It does not claim that model experiments have been conducted, that the challenge examples have been independently corrected, or that the proposed research gap is novel. The task is understood as sequential array optimization—not database ACID processing, multithreaded concurrency, or distributed-ledger consistency.
